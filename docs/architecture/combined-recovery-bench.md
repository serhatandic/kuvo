## Encrypted export timing on macOS

The original 50-second outer deadline expired on `98d2c6014` and `acd8ea34c` after the primary durably accepted the prepared export's generation. Acceptance leaves a separate delivery step. The primary offers the removal again and answers the source's challenge with a signed instruction for that generation. The source checks the generation before recording the wipe approval. Its detached worker verifies the export, stops the writers, checks the generation again and authenticates the export immediately before erasing the source folder.

An isolated diagnostic run with an 85-second outer deadline and the unchanged `acd8ea34c` Box binary completed in 84.5 seconds. It reached source detach, delivery acknowledgement and replacement restore, and compared every promised attachment byte. The original 50-second result remains a timeout. A second run, with only the outer deadline relaxed, hit the fixture's 12-second detach deadline despite the source's durable `prepared: true` approval. A diagnostic probe observed the primary acknowledgement at +50.233 s, the source's signed instruction journal at +53.590 s, writer stop beginning at +59.653 s, and link exit at +61.870 s. Erase began before +63.802 s, when the probe observed the source journal had disappeared. The probe's read raced that erase and failed with ENOENT.

The fixture now allows 120 seconds for the whole journey and 30 seconds from primary acknowledgement to detach. The detach deadline allows time for a new Relay offer, archive readback and the worker's writer-stop window. The 25-second preparation, 10-second completion and 8-second replacement replication deadlines and all wipe and byte assertions remain in place. Milestones carry monotonic elapsed times.

With the detach deadline corrected, a macOS run recorded source detach and wipe completion, then timed out awaiting the replacement's ownership replica.
